About Scoutly

About Scoutly

Scoutly is an SEO and search visibility platform in early development. The goal is simple: bring SEO work that’s usually spread across several tools (audits, link checks, backlink research, keyword research, competitor research and AI search visibility) into one workspace that explains what it finds.

The mark

A lens and a spark

SEO often feels like detective work: following link trails, checking clues in the page source, and working out why something isn’t showing up. The Scoutly mark is a search lens, the investigation itself, done patiently and explained clearly.

Inside the lens is a spark: what the investigation turns up, and the AI answers a well-built site gets cited in. Search on the outside, discovery on the inside.

How we build

Principles behind Scoutly

Evidence over scores

Every finding should show what was detected and how to verify it, not just a number to trust.

Labeled data sources

Crawl findings, connected accounts and third-party estimates are kept clearly apart, so you know what you’re looking at.

No guarantees we can’t keep

No tool can guarantee rankings or AI citations. Scoutly won’t claim otherwise.

Plain-English explanations

Findings should make sense to business owners as well as SEO specialists.
