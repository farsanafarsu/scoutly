Backlink Audit

Check the links pointing to your site: which ones still exist, which are nofollow or sponsored, what anchor text they use, and which come from risky pages. Then download a disavow file for Google if you need one.

Where to get your links (free):
  1. Open Google Search Console → Links.
  2. Under Top linking sites, choose More, then Export external links → Latest links.
  3. Upload that CSV below, or paste the linking page URLs.
