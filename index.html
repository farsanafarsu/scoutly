FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Scoutly and SEO.

New to the terms? See the SEO glossary.

What is Scoutly?

Scoutly is an SEO and search visibility platform designed to bring website auditing, backlink analysis, broken link detection, keyword research, competitor research, and supported AI search visibility insights into one workspace.

What does an SEO audit tool do?

An SEO audit tool examines a website for technical and on-page issues that may affect crawling, indexability, site structure, content optimization, and user experience. It helps identify problems and organize potential improvements. See what Scoutly’s website SEO audit checks.

Can Scoutly find broken links?

Yes. Every website audit checks up to 120 internal and external links found on the pages it crawls, and reports errors, redirects and links that couldn’t be verified. Results depend on crawl limits, website access, and server responses.

Can Scoutly check backlinks?

Scoutly can analyze backlink information available through its configured data sources. Comprehensive backlink discovery generally requires an external backlink index or provider, and no provider is connected in the current early-access build.

Does Scoutly help with competitor analysis?

Yes. The intended workflow includes comparing available keyword, content, and backlink data to identify opportunities for your website. The depth of analysis depends on the data sources connected.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization, while GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. Both focus on improving how useful content can be understood and surfaced in answer-oriented and generative search experiences. Read more about AEO and GEO.

Can Scoutly guarantee higher Google rankings?

No SEO platform can guarantee specific rankings. Scoutly can help identify issues and opportunities, but outcomes depend on your website, competition, content, technical implementation, and search engine systems.

Is Scoutly suitable for agencies?

Scoutly is intended to support agency workflows involving multiple website audits, backlink research, competitor analysis, and reporting. Project limits and collaboration features will depend on the plan, and will be published before launch.

Does Scoutly offer a free website audit?

The website audit works today. It fetches the page you enter, reads robots.txt and your XML sitemap, crawls up to 25 internal pages up to three clicks deep, checks up to 120 links, and adds Core Web Vitals from Google PageSpeed Insights. Create a free account to run audits and keep your results. Pricing and any free-tier limits will be published before they apply.

How often should I audit my website?

Run an audit when launching a new website or making significant changes, and repeat it periodically to detect new technical issues. Websites that change frequently may benefit from scheduled crawls.
