    Google Business Profile

    Checklist (Google doesn’t let tools read it)

    • Claim and verify your profile at business.google.com.
    • Use exactly the same name, address and phone as your website.
    • Pick the most specific primary category, then add secondary ones.
    • Add opening hours, holiday hours, services, and at least 10 real photos.
    • Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to every review.
    • Post updates or offers at least monthly.
    • Link the profile to the matching location page on your website.
