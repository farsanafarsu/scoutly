Site audit

SEO audit

Enter any page. Scoutly fetches it, reads robots.txt and your sitemap, crawls up to 25 internal pages, checks every link and scores what it finds, then adds Core Web Vitals from Google PageSpeed Insights.

The page to audit. https:// is added if you leave it out.
Optional. Adds keyword checks for title, headings and more.
 

  • Crawlability & indexingStatus codes, redirects, HTTPS, robots.txt, sitemap, noindex, canonical and hreflang.
  • On-page SEOTitles, meta descriptions, headings, viewport, language, URL and duplicates across pages.
  • Content & keywordsWord count, image alt text, and where your keyword appears on the page.
  • Links & redirectsBroken internal and external links, redirecting links, anchor text and nofollow.
  • Internal linkingClick depth, weakly linked pages and sitemap pages nothing links to.
  • Speed & securityCore Web Vitals, response time, compression, render-blocking scripts, images, HSTS.
  • Structured data & socialJSON-LD validity and required properties, Open Graph and X cards.
  • AI search readinessAI crawler access, content without JavaScript, answer structure and llms.txt.
