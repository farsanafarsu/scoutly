“Search” crawlers fetch pages to answer people right now; blocking them keeps you out of those answers. “Training” crawlers collect data for future models; blocking them is a policy choice and doesn’t stop citations. The live test requests your page as that crawler, which catches firewalls (like Cloudflare’s “block AI bots”) that robots.txt doesn’t show.
