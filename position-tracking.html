Position Tracking

Track where your site ranks in Google using your own Search Console data: real positions, clicks and impressions. Import a fresh export every week or month and Scoutly shows what moved. Imports are saved to your account.

Get your data from Google Search Console (free):
  1. Open Search Console → Performance → Search results.
  2. Pick a date range (e.g. last 28 days), turn on Average position, then Export → Download CSV.
  3. Unzip it and drop Queries.csv (and optionally Pages.csv) below.
