AI search visibility

AI search visibility: see how your brand shows up in AI answers

More people now ask AI assistants and AI-powered search features to explain topics, compare products and recommend options. Scoutly’s AI search visibility tracking is designed to show whether your brand is mentioned in those answers, which of your pages are cited, and how that changes over time, on platforms where reliable measurement is possible.

Planned

AI visibility tracking isn’t available in this build. Supported platforms and measurement methods will be listed here before launch.

What it measures

Four signals of AI search visibility

Brand mentions

Whether your brand or product is named in answers to the questions that matter to you.

Citations

Whether your pages are linked or listed as sources, and which URLs are cited most often.

Accuracy

Whether the description of your brand is correct and up to date, so you can fix the source content when it isn’t.

Competitive share

How often you appear compared with competitors for the same set of questions.

How measurement works

AI answers change. Measure trends, not single answers.

Unlike a traditional ranking, an AI-generated answer can be different every time. It depends on the exact wording of the question, the user’s location and conversation, personalization and the model version.

That’s why AI visibility tracking works from samples: a defined set of prompts, run repeatedly, with results recorded over time.

  • A prompt set you define around your topics, products and competitors.
  • Repeated runs to smooth out differences between individual answers.
  • Recorded answers and sources, so every data point can be checked.
  • Clear labels for which platforms were measured, and when.

Limits we’re upfront about

What AI visibility tracking can’t do

  • It can’t see the answers every individual user receives, only the samples that were measured.
  • AI platforms don’t publish complete data about mentions or citations, so all tracking is an estimate.
  • Results reflect the prompts you track. Questions outside your prompt set aren’t covered.
  • It can’t guarantee, buy or force citations. Nobody can.

Improving visibility

Visibility starts with clear, crawlable, trustworthy content

No switch makes AI systems cite you. Google says the same SEO best practices apply to its AI features in Search, with no extra technical requirements: a page needs to be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet. Clear, well-structured content from a source people trust is a sound starting point everywhere.

FAQ

AI search visibility questions

Can Scoutly guarantee that AI tools will cite my website?

No. No tool can guarantee placement in AI-generated answers. Scoutly is designed to measure visibility where it can and to help you improve the content and technical foundations that make your pages easier to understand.

Which AI platforms will Scoutly track?

Supported platforms will be announced once measurement methods are confirmed. Scoutly will only list platforms it can measure reliably and in line with each platform’s terms.

How is AI visibility different from rank tracking?

Rank tracking records the position of a URL in a list of results. AI visibility looks at whether your brand is mentioned and your pages are cited in a generated answer, which has no fixed positions and changes between runs.

Do I need to do anything special for Google’s AI features?

According to Google’s guidance, no. The same SEO best practices apply: pages need to be crawlable, indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet. There’s no special markup or file required.
