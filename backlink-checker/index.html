Backlink checker

Backlink checker for referring domains, lost links and link gaps

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. They help search engines discover your pages and understand how sites relate to each other. Scoutly’s backlink checker is designed to show which domains link to you, which links have disappeared, and which relevant sites link to your competitors but not to you.

Where backlink data comes from

Why crawling your own site can’t find your backlinks

Crawling your website shows the links on your pages, which are the links going out. Backlinks live on other people’s websites. To find them, a tool needs an index built by crawling a large part of the web and recording which pages link where.

Scoutly is being built to use an external backlink data provider for this. That has a few consequences worth knowing:

  • Coverage depends on the provider. No index contains every link on the web, which is why different backlink tools report different totals.
  • Freshness depends on recrawls. New and lost links show up after the provider revisits the linking page.
  • Search Console is a useful second source. Its Links report shows top linking sites that Google has found for your property.

What you can review

Understand your link profile, then act on it

Referring domains

The distinct websites that link to you. Many links from one site usually count for less than links from several relevant sites.

Backlinks and anchor text

Which of your pages attract links, which pages link to them, and the clickable text those links use.

Lost and broken backlinks

Links that disappeared, or links pointing to URLs on your site that now return a 404. These can often be recovered with a redirect or a short email.

Competitor link gap

Domains that link to one or more competitors but not to you. These are often a good place to start relevant outreach.

Outreach prospects

Organize relevant prospects with notes and a status, so outreach stays focused on sites where a link would make sense to their readers.

Change monitoring

Compare snapshots when fresh data is available to see new and lost referring domains over time.

Competitor backlink gap

Find sites that link to competitors but not to you

A link gap analysis compares referring domains across your site and a few competitors. Domains that link to several competitors, but not to you, are worth a closer look: they already cover your topic and link to sites like yours.

Not every gap is an opportunity. A directory that links to everyone, or a site serving a different market, may not be relevant. Treat the list as research, not a to-do list. To choose which competitors to compare, start with SEO competitor analysis.

Link-building guidance

Earn links that make sense to readers

Search engines use links as signals, and they also have rules against manipulating them. Google’s spam policies, for example, list buying or selling links that pass ranking credit and excessive link exchanges as link spam.

  • Create resources people want to cite: original data, useful tools and clear guides.
  • Reclaim lost links by fixing broken URLs or redirecting moved pages.
  • Ask sites that already mention your brand without linking whether they’d add a link.
  • Mark paid or sponsored placements with rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow".

Scoutly helps you research and organize opportunities. It doesn’t buy, sell or place links.

FAQ

Backlink checker questions

How does Scoutly find backlinks?

Backlinks are found on other websites, so Scoutly is designed to read them from an external backlink index rather than from a crawl of your own site. The provider and its coverage will be shown alongside the data.

Why do backlink tools show different numbers?

Each backlink index crawls a different part of the web on a different schedule, and each one counts and filters links differently. Compare trends within one source rather than comparing totals across sources.

What is a lost backlink?

A link that a data source found before but can’t find anymore. The linking page may have been removed or edited, or the URL it pointed to on your site may have changed. Some lost links can be recovered.

Are nofollow links worthless?

Not necessarily. Google treats rel="nofollow", rel="sponsored" and rel="ugc" as hints rather than strict rules, and these links can still send visitors and help people discover your site.

Does Scoutly build links for me?

No. Scoutly is a research and organization tool. It helps you find relevant opportunities and keep track of outreach, but it doesn’t buy, sell or place links.
